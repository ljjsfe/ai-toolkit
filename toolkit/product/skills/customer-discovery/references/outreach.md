# Outreach messages

Read when writing a cold message, an intro request, a follow-up or a confirmation to get or
continue a customer conversation.

## Rules for every message

- Under 120 words. Never link to or describe the product; never ask for "feedback on my
  idea".
- The subject line is about their work ("how you handle driver scheduling"), not about you
  ("quick question", "startup idea").
- Every detail is true. If the founder has not given you a real open question or a real
  reason this person is worth hearing from, ask for both in one line before writing — never
  invent them (the same rule as the `writing` skill's no-invention law).

## Cold message — five parts

1. **What you are working on** — the specific pain, for the specific role.
2. **Not selling** — say plainly you are early and trying to understand the problem before
   building the wrong thing.
3. **One honest unknown** — a concrete thing you cannot yet tell, not "I'd love to learn
   more".
4. **Why them** — one specific, true fact about their experience; never generic praise.
5. **A small ask** — e.g. 15 minutes to hear how it works at their company.

## Other messages

- **Warm intro:** two lines to the introducer, then a paragraph they can forward that carries
  the same five parts in the third person.
- **After a conversation:** quote one specific thing they said and how it changed your view;
  if they named someone, ask for that intro with a forwardable note; otherwise ask who else
  you should talk to. No pitch, no "let me show you what we're building".
- **After a commitment:** same day, restate what they committed to and the date, what you
  will bring, and who else should attend.
- **A lead gone quiet:** one attempt only — one concrete ask with a cost attached, and say a
  "no" helps too. No reply: archive it.

## Output

The message, then two or three lines: which of the five parts is weakest and what real detail
would strengthen it. Reviewing a draft: mark each sentence that pitches, flatters generically
or asks for an opinion, and rewrite it.
