# Full evidence ladder and interview scorecard

Read when debriefing: the three tiers in Step 3 decide the gate; the rung number decides
which fact is strongest, and the scorecard decides how far to trust the notes at all.

## The 19 rungs

Each fact gets a rung; report the count at rung 8 or above and the highest rung reached.
Higher is costlier for the customer to produce, so it outranks a lower rung inside a tier.

| rung | signal | tier |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | compliment | no evidence |
| 2 | opinion | no evidence |
| 3 | intention ("I would") | no evidence |
| 4 | hypothetical price ("I'd pay $X") | no evidence |
| 5 | feature request (keep its "why"; the why may be a rung-8 fact) | no evidence |
| 6 | generic complaint ("always", "often") | no evidence |
| 7 | agreement with a problem the interviewer named | no evidence |
| 8 | specific past incident, dated | problem is real |
| 9 | emotional recounting of that incident | problem is real |
| 10 | named consequence (lost deal, overtime, fine) | problem is real |
| 11 | specific objection to a current tool | problem is real |
| 12 | active search for a fix | problem is real |
| 13 | workaround they built | problem is real |
| 14 | time already invested in the problem | problem is real |
| 15 | money already spent on the problem | problem is real |
| 16 | dated time committed to you | solution is wanted |
| 17 | intro to the decision-maker | solution is wanted |
| 18 | resources committed (data, staff, pilot slot) | solution is wanted |
| 19 | money paid to you | solution is wanted |

Where the rungs change the grading beyond the tiers:

- Emotion (9) counts only attached to a dated incident; emotion about a hypothetical is 2.
- A feature request (5) is zero, but ask the why: the incident behind it is graded on its own.
- Toolkit policy (not in the Mom Test skills): a segment where every interview tops out at 8–9
  and none reaches 12–15 may have a real but cheap problem; say so in the gate line, since a
  priced test is then the next thing to check.
- Toolkit policy (not in the Mom Test skills): tie-breaks in the gate use the highest rung, so
  one rung-19 payment outweighs any number of rung-16 meetings for a solution assumption.

## Interview scorecard (1 / 3 / 5 per row, out of 75)

Scores the conversation, not the customer. Score only what the notes show; a row the notes
cannot judge is "unknown" and becomes a capture instruction for the next call.

| # | row | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | talk ratio | interviewer talked most | customer talked 70% or more |
| 2 | pitching | pitched early | no pitch, or only after discovery ended |
| 3 | behaviour questions | mostly opinions and hypotheticals | all past or present behaviour |
| 4 | leading | interviewer named the problem | customer named problems unprompted |
| 5 | current solution | not asked | what, cost, what is wrong, why not switched |
| 6 | costs | no figures | time, money and consequence figures |
| 7 | frequency | "often" | a count plus the last instances |
| 8 | urgency | not asked | trigger or deadline known, or its absence |
| 9 | specific examples | none | two or more dated incidents |
| 10 | what they tried | not asked | attempts and why each failed |
| 11 | facts vs opinions | blurred | every fact backed by a quote |
| 12 | surprise | nothing new | something moved an assumption |
| 13 | scary question | avoided | asked early, real answer |
| 14 | compliments | logged as positive | deflected and anchored |
| 15 | next step | "keep in touch" | dated commitment or a clear no, plus a new name |

Bands: 15–30 no evidence; 31–45 facts but not actionable; 46–60 solid; 61–75 expert (check
you are not grading generously). Give the total, the two lowest rows and one change for the
next call.

Where the scorecard changes the grading (toolkit policy; the source scores interviews but does
not tie the score to the gate):

- **Leading scored 1:** every problem fact the interviewer introduced drops to rung 7 unless
  the customer then gave a dated incident of their own.
- **Pitching scored 1:** commitments made after a pitch still count, but problem facts given
  after the pitch are checked for prompted agreement first.
- **Total under 31:** the interview does not count toward the three-interview bar of the
  gate, whatever its facts say; rerun with the same person or segment.
