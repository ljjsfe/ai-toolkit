# Regulatory and contractual clocks

Read when an incident may involve personal data, a listed company or contractual notice terms.
Confirm the current rule with legal; these are starting points, not legal advice.

- **GDPR:** a controller notifies the supervisory authority without undue delay and where
  feasible within 72 hours of becoming aware (unless unlikely to risk people's rights), and
  tells affected people when the risk is high; a processor — typical for B2B SaaS — notifies its
  controller (the customer) without undue delay.
- **US public companies:** SEC 8-K within 4 business days of determining materiality.
- **US state breach laws**, sector rules and customer contracts add their own clocks; list every
  one that applies at the start of the incident.
