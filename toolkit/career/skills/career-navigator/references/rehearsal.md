# Rehearsal — practise the conversation before you have it

Knowing the script is not the same as saying it to your boss's face and holding the silence.
Rehearse any conversation where you are asking for something, pushing back, or using leverage.

## Set up (ask in one pass)

- Who you are talking to: role, style (results / process / anxious / funnel / umbrella), what
  they want, what pressure they are under. Take it from the dossier's people table when it is
  there.
- What you want from this conversation: one decision, stated as the sentence you would accept.
- Your leverage, from the inventory, and the card you are willing to play — or not.

## Play

- Play the counterpart as they really are, not as a cooperative partner: stall, deflect ("let's
  revisit next quarter"), promise vaguely, push back, go quiet, get irritated. Use the moves
  that person actually uses.
- One turn at a time. The user speaks in English, as they would in the room.
- Don't coach during the round. Stop after the user gets a decision, gives up, or 8 turns.

## Debrief (after each round)

Score 1–5 on each, with the line that earned the score:

| | what it means |
|---|---|
| the ask | did you say exactly what you want, without "if possible", "maybe"? |
| leverage | did you use what you have — and not imply what you don't have? |
| warm / firm | friendly in tone, unmoved in substance? |
| silence | after the key sentence, did you stop talking? |
| concessions | did you give anything away you didn't have to? |

Then: the three sentences to cut, and a rewritten version of the best line. Run a second round
with the counterpart a notch harder. Stop when two rounds in a row score 4 or more on the ask
and on warm / firm.

## After the real conversation

Log it in the dossier's cards-played table, then run the after-action review in `dossier.md`.
